# Kerf — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Kerf does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login.
- **No tracking.** There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
  SDK that sends data anywhere.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Calculations, the calculator tape, tool
  inputs and settings are stored in the app's own private storage on your
  device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Purchases.** The Kerf Pro unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple
  processes the payment; Kerf only receives confirmation from the App Store
  that the purchase exists, so it can unlock the tools. Kerf never sees your
  payment details or your Apple Account.
- **Sharing is only what you share.** If you tap Share on a result, the text is
  handed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you send it.
