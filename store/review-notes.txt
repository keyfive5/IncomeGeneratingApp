Kerf is a construction calculator: tape-measure arithmetic in feet, inches and fractions, plus fifteen jobsite tools that turn measurements into cuts (stairs, rafters, stud layout marks, baluster spacing, crown molding saw angles, concrete and more).

HOW TO SEE IT IN 30 SECONDS
1. Calculator tab: press 5, ft, 7, in, 3, ⁄, 8, +, 2, ft, 1, 1, in, 5, ⁄, 1, 6, =. The result is 8' 6 11/16", with every other reading (inches, decimal feet, mm) underneath.
2. Tools tab > Concrete (free): change Length to 20' and the cubic yards and bag counts update.
3. Tools tab > Stairs (Pro): the tool opens with a worked example and a drawing. Tapping any field opens the Kerf Pro purchase sheet.

IN-APP PURCHASE
One non-consumable, com.hasanzafar.kerf.pro ("Kerf Pro"), unlocks the eleven Pro tools for good. It is not a subscription. The purchase sheet is reached from any Pro tool, the banner at the top of the Tools tab, or Settings > Unlock Kerf Pro. Restore Purchase is on the purchase sheet and in Settings. Free with no limits: the calculator, Concrete, Right Triangle & Square, and Slope & Grade.

No account, no login, no ads. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only connection is StoreKit for the purchase.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- Its own expression engine for tape-measure arithmetic that tracks dimension: length × length is square feet, × a thickness is cubic yards, length ÷ length is a count. Results are rounded to 1/8–1/64" and marked "strong" or "scant" the way carpenters read a tape.
- Tools give the marks to pull, not just a total: every stud layout mark from the end of the plate, every baluster edge from the post, framing square settings for a stair stringer, and compound miter and bevel for crown at any spring angle and corner.
- Code and comfort checks: IRC riser and tread limits, the 4" sphere rule for guards, minimum drain slope.
- 220 automated tests check the engine and every tool against hand-worked jobsite numbers.
